Can the people who use this product get through the change without getting
stuck or confused?

Look at it running if you can: start the dev server from the brief on your port
and use the page-viewing approach the brief describes. Don't sign in with real
accounts, submit forms that reach real services, or change data. If you can't
run it, review the code and templates, and list what you couldn't see under
Notes in your review.

- **Flow**: it does what the spec promises from start to finish, with no dead
  ends or surprising jumps.
- **States**: loading, empty, error and success states exist and tell the
  person what happened and what they can do next.
- **People**: each kind of user the change reaches (the brief's project notes
  list them) gets a sensible path, consistent with how the app treats them
  elsewhere.
- **Words**: labels, messages and buttons are short, plain, specific, and
  consistent with the app's existing wording.
- **Accessibility**: real buttons and links, labelled inputs, a sensible focus
  order, keyboard operation, text alternatives for images, and no meaning
  carried by colour alone.
- **Small screens**: it works at phone width.

Use CHANGES for problems a real user would hit within the spec's scope. Taste
goes under Notes.
